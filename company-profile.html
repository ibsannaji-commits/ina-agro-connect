About the Company

INA Natural Products PLC is a leading Ethiopian producer and supplier of high-quality agricultural products. We work closely with smallholder farmers and cooperatives across Oromia, Amhara and Sidama regions to source premium coffee, moringa, rosemary, spices and other botanicals for domestic and international B2B buyers.

Our focus is on traceability, consistent quality and reliable supply. All products undergo internal quality checks and we support third-party inspection when required by buyers.
