How the RFQ process works

  1. You submit a detailed Request for Quotation.
  2. Matching verified suppliers receive the RFQ and can respond with price, quantity, lead time and terms.
  3. You compare quotes, negotiate via messaging, and select the best offer.
  4. Accepted quotation can be converted into a formal order on the platform.
