About INA Agro Connect

Connecting Ethiopian agriculture to local and global markets

Our Mission

INA Agro Connect is a B2B agricultural marketplace that helps verified Ethiopian producers, cooperatives and exporters connect directly with buyers across Ethiopia, Africa and the world. We reduce middlemen, increase transparency and open new markets for quality agricultural products.

What We Offer

  • ✓ Verified suppliers and buyers
  • ✓ Product listings & search
  • ✓ RFQ and negotiation tools
  • ✓ Messaging and deal tracking
  • ✓ Subscription plans for suppliers (Free / Pro / Premium)
  • ✓ Web + Mobile (Android & iOS) platforms

Focus Products

Coffee, Sesame, Moringa, Spices, Pulses, Honey, Rosemary, Teff, Fruits, Vegetables and more.

Vision

From Ethiopian farms to global markets — building the trusted digital infrastructure for African agricultural trade.
